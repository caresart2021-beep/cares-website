Troop Service Project

March 2024

A troop joined us for a service project, painting copies of our “Burst of Blooms” design together. Working side by side, they turned out a whole set of bright paintings of hummingbirds, birdhouses, and sunflowers.

Thank you to everyone who gave their time to make art for others!
