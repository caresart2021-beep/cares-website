Irvington High School Wellness Center

April 2022

Our officers hosted a paint by number event in the Irvington High School Wellness Center, inviting students to relax and de-stress during Flex. Everyone painted a jellyfish design, one numbered color at a time.

It was a calming break in the school day, and we loved painting alongside our classmates!
