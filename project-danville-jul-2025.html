Danville Senior Center

July 2025

We hosted a lighthouse painting workshop at Danville Senior Center. Everyone began with soft washes of sky and ocean, then built up the lighthouse and the sand and grass in front of it.

No two paintings looked alike, and the table full of finished lighthouses at the end was a joy to see.
