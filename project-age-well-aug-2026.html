Age Well Center at South Fremont

August 2026

We returned to the Age Well Center at South Fremont to paint a glowing sunset over the water. Participants built the painting up one layer at a time, blending warm oranges and pinks into a deep blue sea.

We were so happy to see everyone holding up their finished paintings with big smiles.
