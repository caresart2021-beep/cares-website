At Novelle we tried something a little different: a free-form painting day. Instead of following one design, residents chose their own colors and brushstrokes.

The tables filled up with bold, expressive pieces, from sweeping abstract strokes to colorful landscapes. We loved seeing how personal every painting turned out!
