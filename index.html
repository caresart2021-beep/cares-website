About CARES

We are CARES, a non-profit art organization located in Fremont, CA, dedicated to creating paintings for healthcare facilities and hosting art workshops for seniors and neurodivergent communities.

With the onset of the pandemic, many healthcare facilities were overwhelmed with work and there was an overall toll on mental health. In an attempt to help the health care community during the pandemic, Ananya and Advika came up with the idea of CARES to form the organization in 2021. Since then, our team has been working towards creating colorful art with the help of the community.

Just as important, we want CARES to be a space that uplifts patients too, where making art together can bring a little joy to a hard day.
