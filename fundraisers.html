Fundraisers

Our fundraisers keep CARES running, and each one helps us bring more art to healthcare communities.

South Indian Food Sale

July 2025

Our team cooked up a fundraiser by selling South Indian food that we made ourselves, from prepping the vegetables to packaging and labeling each order. We raised over $800, and all of it goes straight into our operations!

Halloween Art Workshop

October 2024

Our team holds interactive workshops where we teach elementary and middle school students new art styles and techniques while working on a festive art piece. In our October workshop, we taught several students how to use different pencil shading and blending techniques while drawing a Halloween-themed sketch. The donations collected from these workshops go towards funding supplies that we use to create our paintings for healthcare facilities. We raised over $150!
