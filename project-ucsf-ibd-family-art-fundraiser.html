UCSF IBD Family Art Fundraiser

November 2023

We were proud to take part in an art fundraiser for patients and families of the UCSF Inflammatory Bowel Disease program. We brought supplies and led a step-by-step painting class, and everyone painted a moonlit lavender field.

Our own paintings were displayed on easels around the room, too.
