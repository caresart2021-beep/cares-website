Age Well Center at South Fremont

July 2025

This time we painted a seaside lighthouse at the Age Well Center at South Fremont. Participants started with a blue sky and ocean, then added the striped lighthouse and tall dune grass on top.

We were so excited to see how proud everyone was of their finished paintings!
